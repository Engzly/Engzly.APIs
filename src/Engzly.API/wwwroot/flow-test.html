3 Login

Exchanges email+password for an access token. The token is stored per-role and used for every subsequent authenticated request and SignalR connection.

4 Categories & AI classify

List the catalog, then let the AI pick the best category for the task title you're about to publish.

5 Publish gig client

Creates a task with the selected category. The returned gigId drives the rest of the flow.

6 Submit & approve proposal

Helper sends a proposal on the gig; client views pending proposals and approves one (decision=1). Approval creates the gig conversation and adds both users as participants.

8 Chat: list, open, send

List each user's conversations — the gig conversation should appear for both. Opening it joins the hub group and loads history. Sending a message also stores messageId so you can edit/delete it in step 9.

10 Image & location messages

Image uploads go through multipart/form-data and are stored under /wwwroot/chat. Location sends raw lat/lng to /messages/location.

11 Chatbot (client asks FAQ)

Opens / reuses a private bot conversation for the client and asks the curated Engzly FAQ assistant (Python embedding-based).

12 Remove helper from gig client

Soft-leaves the helper in the gig conversation with a reason and drops the assignment. The helper can no longer post in the chat after this.

13 Complete, verify, delete

Gig lifecycle wrap-up. Complete marks it done, verify flips it to verified, delete permanently removes it.
